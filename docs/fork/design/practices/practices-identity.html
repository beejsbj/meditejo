Ways to practise: names and identities

Phase 1 of Burooj's note on the pills (2026-11-02): “Cards/tiles could allow us the og language with a small description. Each meditation technique could be given identity.” Nothing in the app has changed yet. This page shows each of the 36 practices with its name in its own language, a figure drawn by Meditejo's art engine that says something true about the practice, and three ways to lay out the tiles, in Tidewater light and dark at the S23+'s width (384 dp). Everything animates; the clock is the breath (10 s).

What I’d build: B on Explore (one carousel per group: the figures carry the identity, the groups teach the map, and Explore stays a front door rather than a wall of 36), with a “See all” that opens A as the practices page, and C in the Labels sheet, where density matters more than words. All three share one tile anatomy (figure, original name, tradition · script, one line), so it is one component in three sizes. The names table is ready to go into the asset; the ambiguous ones (Forgiveness, Noting, Chanting, Devotion, Movement, Visualisation) are yours to settle.

All 36 figures

Each figure is curated, not random (decision 22): one figure and one ground per practice, in the engine's own contract (build / draw, a calm still frame, seamless loops), so Phase 2 ports them beside the Dart figures. Breath, grass and the lotus are round 4's primitives as they are; the other 33 are new.

Names

The tile shows the name in the practice's own language, its tradition, and its script where the tradition writes it (Pāli has no single script, so it stays romanised). Practices born in English keep their English names. The English name stays as an alias, so typing “loving-kindness” still finds Mettā. Contested or ambiguous ones say so in the last column.
