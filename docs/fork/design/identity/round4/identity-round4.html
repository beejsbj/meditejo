Meditejo · identity · round 4 · art

Places, chosen by hand

Round 2's atmospheres return as grounds; round 3's strongest figures sit on them; a tight meaning map says which may meet. What Meditejo ships is curated: every built-in sound, preset and starter course has a hand-picked card below, and you can change any pick and export the file. What people add themselves is generated by the same rules.

01

Grounds: the atmospheres

Ten grounds, each moving slowly and looping without a seam. With reduced motion every card shows a still frame.

02

Figures

Seventeen, each with one graceful motion. Dropped from round 3: dot rhythm, strata, stack, grove, weave, the moon row and the sun as its own figure (it is now the disc's rayed kind). The constellation is now a star shape: bright stars placed to suggest a dipper, a W, a cross, a swan, a lotus or a ring, with nothing joining them.

03

Ground, figure, together

For four curated items: the atmosphere alone, the figure on flat paper, and the two together as shipped.

04

The meaning map

Only these may meet. A variant after a colon is pinned: rings:ripple is always a ripple. Noise takes texture grounds only.

05

Curation

Every shipped item with eight candidates from its row of the map. The ringed one is the current pick; first pass marks the pick committed in curated_art.json. Tap a candidate to choose it, then export.

The exported file (copy it if the download is blocked)
06

Not shipped: generated by rule

Anything a person adds, imports or creates is built from its id by the same map, so it is stable and fits. Dharma Seed retreats and teachers in Explore change with the catalogue, so they follow the course and teacher rows rather than a hand pick (a teacher's own photo, when there is one, wins).

Sources in round4/: grounds.js, prims.js, compose.js (meaning map, candidates, freeze), shipped.js, export.js; picks in picks.json, frozen by curate.mjs into curated_art.json. Porting notes: NOTES.md.
