Meditejo · identity · round 5 · places and bells

The rooms we listen from

Rain on a window, a balcony, a car roof; a café, a laundry room, a metro carriage. Round 5 gives the modern sounds their own places and things, gives the noises their colours, and draws every bell that ships from the same five controls that make its sound. Round 4's approved picks are unchanged.

01

New grounds: places

A window with rain on the glass and the room's light behind it; a balcony railing or a terrace floor against the city; a car's windscreen with drops that bead and slide; a metro window with lights passing; a room with a lamp's warm pool.

02

New figures: things in them

Each with one slow motion: the lamp's light breathes, steam drifts, the porthole turns, the fan's blades turn, the umbrella sways and drips, drops slide, straps swing with the carriage, a door lets out a little light.

03

Noise in its colour

White is bright near-white, pink a soft pink, brown a deep brown grain, each leaning a little toward the palette. Still texture only. The Stillness preset, which is mostly brown noise, now shows as brown too (its frozen parameters are unchanged).

04

Bells

The eleven bells that ship, as cards. Each is still until you tap it; then it is struck once and moves as its sound does. Its size comes from its pitch, its rim and highlight from its brightness, how long it moves from its ring, how hard it is knocked from its strike, and the rim's flicker from its beat. The numbers are read from tools/meditejo/generate_bells.py; the Swell bell is a recording, so its numbers are measured.

05

Make a bell

A prototype of the custom-bell editor (BJS-587): start from any bell, move the five controls, and the figure redraws as you drag. Let go to strike it. The sound here is a browser sketch of the same model, not the shipped file.
