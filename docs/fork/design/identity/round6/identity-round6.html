Meditejo · identity · round 6 · the house mark

An M in the roof

The minimal shelter, m-2026-10-26, with the roof's top corner folded in. The two slopes are the shipped ones, unchanged; they stop at two shoulders, and the apex dips between them, so the roof over the pebble also reads as an M for Meditejo. All in Tidewater.

Each variant

384 px as the square icon (iOS, the legacy mipmaps); 48 and 36 px in Android's circle and squircle masks, which show the adaptive icon's central 72 dp; on light and dark home screens; and as Home's mark, breathing: the pebble moves between round and organic over ten seconds. Tap a Home mark to watch the roof spread again.

03

Frozen, ready to drop in

Each variant is a house_mark.json in the format house_mark.dart reads: B's geometry plus up to four new numbers in values. The shipped roof code ignores them, so shelter_notch.patch teaches shelterRoofCommands to draw them.

To ship one

git apply docs/fork/design/identity/round6/shelter_notch.patch   # on fork/identity
cp docs/fork/design/identity/round6/marks/m2-clear.json assets/branding/house_mark.json
uv run tools/meditejo/branding/make_icons.py

Sources in round6/: shelter.js (a port of mark_outline.dart's shelter, plus the notch), variants.json, freeze.mjs (writes marks/*.json and the SVGs house_mark.dart writes from them), shelter_notch.patch. Notes: NOTES.md, round 6.
