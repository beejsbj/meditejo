More to build with

Round 2's cards all came from one recipe per kind, so they read alike. Here the art is built from a library of 22 small primitives (breath, fire, rings, mandalas, strata, a lotus, an ammonite…), layered by a seeded grammar: paper, ground, figure, accent. Marks and palettes are settled and untouched.

01

The primitives

Each is a small seeded generator with one quiet motion. Lists use a still frame. Shown alone, as a figure on plain paper; those marked ground can also fill a card behind another.

02

The grammar

A card is paper, then 0–1 ground, 1 figure, 0–1 accent printed on a second, slightly misregistered plate. The seed picks the structure first (which primitives, which layout, which value key) and only then the details.

Eight rain cards: the same meaning, different structures

03

Sounds and presets

Rain → streaks or ripples; fire → tongues, embers or rays; water → rings, waves or scales; wind → threads; night → constellations and moons; drone → concentric rings; binaural → interference; noise → dot fields. A preset keeps its loudest layer's card and adds each quieter layer's figure as an accent.

04

Covers

Courses take horizons, strata, suns and mandalas; playlists are stacked or woven; teachers get a single mandala or petal portrait; singles are one primitive alone. Seeded by ID, drawn once.
