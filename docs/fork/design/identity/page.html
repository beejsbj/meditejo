Meditejo · identity · round 1

A mark that breathes

Seeded marks in three families. Every seed reproduces exactly; reroll for more. Tap a mark to pause it, Use to try it as the app icon and on Home. The palette recolours everything.

Selected
01

Gesture on form

The Duet direction: one hand-drawn line over an off-centre pebble. Loops, ensō, a breath wave, a spiral, cursive arches that hint at an m, infinity.

02

Atmospheric

Light through mist, a sun over water: translucent layers, glow and grain, but each built on one clear silhouette.

03

Minimal

Two or three flat, slightly hand-cut shapes with clear figure and ground. The most icon-like.

04

Palettes

Mark roles (tile, form, stroke, accent) above; app neutrals for light and dark below. Each shows the selected mark.

05

App icon

Android adaptive icon: the tile is the background layer, the mark the foreground, kept inside the 66 dp safe zone. Real sizes at 48, 96 and 192 px.

Circle mask (Pixel)

Squircle mask (Samsung One UI)

06

On Home

The top of Home with the selected mark and palette, light and dark. On the app's own background the mark has no tile, so a stroke that would vanish switches to the text colour.

To choose, send a seed and a palette, e.g. g-1kx · clay, and what you'd keep or change.
