Meditejo · identity · round 2

A place that breathes

Meditejo: a place for meditation. The marks now evoke a place (a doorway, a shelter, a cushion, a hearth) and every round shape breathes from imperfect to whole and back, once every ten seconds. A new mark arrives each day; pin one to keep it.

Today
01

Gesture, placed

One hand-drawn line and one pebble make a place together: a sun in a doorway, a sitter under a roof or on a cushion, smoke over a hearth, a path that ends where you rest. The double loop is still in the pool, rarely.

02

Minimal

Flat shapes with figure and ground, widened toward place: window, hearth, clearing, cushion, shelter.

03

Atmospheric

Printed rather than rendered: flat inks in two slightly misregistered plates, one soft light, paper tooth and ink speckle.

04

Sounds

Each card is drawn by its sound's generator: rain falls, water braids, the fire flickers, stars turn. Presets blend the elements of their layers by volume. Cards move only while on screen.

05

Covers

Seeded by the item's ID, so a course keeps its cover. Cut paper, two inks slightly out of register, paper grain. Drawn once and cached.

06

Palettes as the app's theme

Each board is the app under one palette: Home's mark and Continue card, two Sounds cards and a Library row. Tap a board to apply it, or choose Daily to let the date pick.
